# Receive a wallet transfer by QR

Payments · 2 min · External references checked 2026-09-12.

Create a QR that asks a compatible wallet to send USDC or USDT to an address you control. This is a wallet top-up, not an escrow payment.

## 1. Use your own address

Enter a wallet address you control and check the full address carefully.

## 2. Choose the right asset

Select the currency and network you actually intend to receive.

## 3. Review the request

The sender should see the same address, currency and amount before approving.

## 4. Check your wallet

Wait for the transfer to appear in your wallet and keep the payment reference.

## 5. Keep the purposes separate

A wallet top-up does not create or fund an Escrow Global deal.

## Important limitation

This website does not monitor real deposits. Never describe a personal receive QR as an escrow deposit address.
