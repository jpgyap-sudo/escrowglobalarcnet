# Complete verification safely

Jupiter Spend · 3 min · External references checked 2026-09-12.

KYC is performed inside Jupiter’s official flow and its verification provider—not by uploading identity documents to this demo.

## 1. Check eligibility first

The documented minimum age is 18. Confirm your actual country of residence and eligibility; do not misstate your location.

## 2. Prepare your own documents

The flow can request a government identity document, phone verification, personal information, a questionnaire and a liveness check. The current APAC flow also requires proof of address.

## 3. Complete the official verification flow

Use the Verification Center and follow the SumSub verification prompts. Keep document information consistent with your real details and use a clear, unedited capture.

## 4. Check status in the app

Manual review may take longer than automated verification. Follow official requests for additional information; no approval or processing time is guaranteed.

## 5. Protect your recovery information

A legitimate KYC request does not need your wallet seed phrase. Never send credentials to Escrow Global, a chat contact or an unofficial “verification agent”.

## Important limitation

Escrow Global does not perform Jupiter KYC, store your identity files, or bypass issuer restrictions.
