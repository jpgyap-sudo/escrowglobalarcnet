# Buy and sell physical products

Products · 4 min · External references checked 2026-09-12.

A tracking number is evidence of a shipment, not proof that the correct item arrived in good condition.

## 1. Check the specification

Agree material, dimensions, model or serial number, quantity, condition, packing, delivery area and shipping charges. For wholesale, specify sample approval and inspection responsibility.

## 2. Reserve stock by funding

A product agreement is not a stock reservation until funding succeeds. The sandbox rechecks inventory at funding to prevent overselling.

## 3. Record shipment

The seller adds carrier, tracking and a packing-evidence reference. Carrier events entered here are seller-reported, not verified logistics API results.

## 4. Confirm receipt, then inspect

Only the buyer confirms receipt to start the inspection window. Receipt itself releases nothing. Approve the delivery milestone only after inspecting against the checklist.

## 5. Handle a return or holdback

A return request is free, but does not automatically refund. Agree logistics, record return shipment, and accept the seller’s refund offer or use formal dispute resolution. A warranty holdback is released only by the buyer after its period, or resolved by agreement/dispute.

## Important limitation

Real addresses and shipment evidence require private storage in production. This demo stores references and optional file digests, not private documents.
