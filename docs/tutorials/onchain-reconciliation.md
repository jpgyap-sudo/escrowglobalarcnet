# How to check a payment status

Payments · 3 min · External references checked 2026-09-12.

Use the deal page and your wallet receipt together. A message saying “approved” is not enough to prove that a payment reached the right place.

## 1. Open the deal

Start with the agreement you intended to fund.

## 2. Check the amount and asset

Make sure the payment matches the agreement and uses the intended network.

## 3. Check the recipient

Confirm the destination shown by your wallet is the one you expected.

## 4. Check the status

Wait for the wallet and deal page to show a completed result. Do not count a pending payment as complete.

## 5. Ask for help when they disagree

Keep the receipt and contact support instead of trying the payment repeatedly.

## Important limitation

This website is an interactive sandbox. Balances and identities are examples only; no real funds move here. This website does not monitor real deposits or automatically credit real funds.
