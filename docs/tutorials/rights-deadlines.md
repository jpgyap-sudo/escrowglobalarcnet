# Know your rights and deadlines

Resolution · 3 min · External references checked 2026-09-12.

Use the deal room to see who can act next, what the current deadline means, and which fallback is still available without confusing a product screen for a guarantee.

## 1. Read the action owner

The page identifies whether the next step belongs to the buyer, seller, reviewer or both parties.

## 2. Read the permitted action

Funding, delivery, approval, revision, dispute, refund and release are different actions.

## 3. Check the clock

Treat delivery and review dates as commitments to follow, not as automatic decisions.

## 4. Prepare the evidence

Tie each claim to the acceptance checklist, delivery record, messages and supporting material.

## 5. Ask before a deadline expires

If you are unsure, contact the other party or support early.

## Important limitation

The rights dashboard is informational. It is not legal advice or a legal deadline notice.
