# Escrow development, marketing and contracts

Better agreements · 4 min · External references checked 2026-09-12.

Define observable work instead of promising a result that neither party controls.

## 1. Development

Name repositories, supported platforms, acceptance tests, source-code ownership, credentials handover and defect period. Break discovery, build and handover into separate milestones.

## 2. Marketing

Specify asset count, placements, dates, approval rights, advertising-spend responsibility and reporting access. Separate the agency fee from ad spend. Never require fake engagement or market manipulation.

## 3. Contracts and procurement

Fund a defined payment obligation—not “the legal contract” itself. Specify deliverables, document versions and a named inspector where necessary. Title transfers and regulated trust obligations need a separate legal framework.

## 4. Deposits and warranty balances

Define what can be deducted, the inspection evidence, time limits and the decision process. Do not advertise interest, insurance or a guaranteed refund.

## 5. Digital handover

List transferable domain or source-asset rights and required handover steps. Do not trade accounts or licences that cannot legally be transferred.

## Important limitation

Templates are workflow starting points, not jurisdiction-specific legal advice. Do not use this demo for securities, regulated escrow, illegal goods or customer funds.
