# Use the Action Center without missing a deadline

Your workspace · 3 min · External references checked 2026-09-12.

The Action Center turns the agreement state into a short, role-specific list of what to do next. It is a guide, not a blockchain notification or legal notice.

## 1. Open My deals

The list puts urgent items first, followed by items that need attention.

## 2. Check your role

Buyers, sellers and reviewers see different prompts.

## 3. Follow the next step

A prompt may ask you to accept, fund, deliver, inspect, revise or respond.

## 4. Open the deal room

Review the agreement and evidence before taking action.

## 5. Refresh after someone acts

The list changes as the other party updates the deal.

## Important limitation

The Action Center is a sandbox convenience. It does not send push notifications, change balances or replace support or legal advice.
