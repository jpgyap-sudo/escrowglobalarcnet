# Register for Jupiter Global / Spend

Jupiter Spend · 3 min · External references checked 2026-09-12.

Jupiter’s current documentation calls the spending product “Spend”. Your DeFi wallet and your Spend balance are different.

## 1. Use Jupiter’s official app entry point

Open the official Jupiter site and follow its app-store links. Check publisher details. Do not use an unsolicited download or a support agent’s link.

## 2. Set up Jupiter ID

In the app, open your profile and choose Set up Jupiter ID. The documented sign-in options include email, Google and Apple.

## 3. Open the Verification Center

Complete the in-app eligibility flow. Available products depend on residency and the issuer assigned to you. Do not assume that holding a wallet means you are eligible for a card.

## 4. Keep the balances distinct

Supported deposits into Spend are converted to USD at deposit. A DeFi wallet balance does not automatically become Spend funds, and Spend is not your Escrow Global escrow.

## Important limitation

Interface labels and availability may change. Follow the current official app and the linked guide. Escrow Global is independent and is not an issuer or authorized Jupiter merchant integration.
