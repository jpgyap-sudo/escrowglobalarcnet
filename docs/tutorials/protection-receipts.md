# Understand what a deal receipt shows

Payments · 3 min · External references checked 2026-09-12.

A receipt helps you review one deal. It is useful evidence, but it is not a guarantee about the item, seller or company.

## 1. Identify the deal

Check the agreement name, parties and current version.

## 2. Check the asset and amount

Confirm the currency, network and amount shown.

## 3. Check the parties

Make sure the buyer, seller and fee details match what you agreed.

## 4. Check the status and time

Look for whether the payment is pending, complete or under review, and when the record was last checked.

## 5. Understand the limits

A receipt is not insurance, a trust score, proof of product quality or a guarantee of payment.

## Important limitation

Receipts in this sandbox are informational records. Keep the original agreement and ask support about anything that looks wrong.
