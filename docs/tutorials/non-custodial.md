# What makes an escrow feel clear

Why Escrow Global · 3 min · External references checked 2026-09-12.

A clear escrow keeps the agreement visible, gives each person a defined role and avoids surprise changes.

## 1. You keep control

Use a wallet you control and review every payment request before approving it.

## 2. Everyone sees the same deal

The item or service, amount, deadline, acceptance checks and remedies are agreed before funding.

## 3. Money stays separate

The agreed amount is kept apart until the next permitted decision. This sandbox only shows that idea with simulated balances.

## 4. A neutral review is possible

If the facts are disputed, both sides can share evidence and ask for a review within the agreement rules.

## 5. Know the tradeoff

Clear rules reduce confusion, but they do not guarantee product quality, delivery or a refund.

## Important limitation

The non-custodial model is a product concept, not a promise of live custody or buyer protection.

## References
