# Design milestones that are easy to approve

Better agreements · 4 min · External references checked 2026-09-12.

A good milestone is small enough to inspect, specific enough to test, and clear about what happens when the result misses the mark.

## 1. Name the outcome

Describe the finished result, format, quantity, owner and handover location.

## 2. Split the work

Use stages that can be accepted independently instead of hiding all risk in one final step.

## 3. Write the test

Add objective acceptance checks and identify the evidence a reviewer can inspect.

## 4. Choose the remedy

Agree revision rounds, cure periods, return logistics or refund conditions before funding.

## 5. Confirm together

Both parties should accept the same version and asset before funding.

## Important limitation

The milestone planner is a sandbox tool for clearer agreements. It is not legal advice and does not guarantee a result.
