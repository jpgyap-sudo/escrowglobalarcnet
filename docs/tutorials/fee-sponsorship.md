# Understand fee support

Access and fees · 3 min · External references checked 2026-09-12.

Some services may help cover a network fee for an approved action. That support is separate from the deal amount and does not change who approves the action.

## 1. Separate the costs

A network fee, platform fee and dispute charge are different amounts.

## 2. Keep your approval

Fee support does not give another person permission to approve or change your payment.

## 3. Check the limits

Read any eligibility, limit or fallback message before continuing.

## 4. Do not repeat a failed payment blindly

If a request fails or times out, check the deal status before trying again.

## 5. Ask for help

Use support if you are unsure whether a fee was paid or a payment was repeated.

## Important limitation

Fee support is not enabled for real payments in this sandbox. Never send tokens or SOL to unlock a remedy.
