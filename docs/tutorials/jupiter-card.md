# Fund and use a Jupiter card

Jupiter Spend · 3 min · External references checked 2026-09-12.

Spend provides a Visa debit-card product for eligible users. Issuer, country support, fees and limits must be checked in the app.

## 1. Check the assigned issuer

Your residence determines the applicable issuer and product availability. Do not assume that two users receive the same card features.

## 2. Follow the exact deposit instructions

In Spend, choose the supported token and network, then copy the displayed address. Confirm with a small transfer when appropriate. Never send unsupported assets or use the Escrow Global demo address.

## 3. Review the USD conversion

Spend converts supported crypto deposits into its USD balance. Check the displayed exchange terms and any fees before depositing.

## 4. Use card controls

View details privately, freeze the card when necessary, and add it to Apple Wallet or Google Wallet only where the app offers that option. Check spending limits and foreign-currency charges.

## 5. Know the payment boundary

A card purchase pays the merchant through card rails. It is not an on-chain Escrow Global escrow funding event. An on-ramp may reject a given card; do not assume the Jupiter card can buy crypto.

## Important limitation

The documentation currently describes digital cards. Do not promise a physical card, cashback level or card approval based on this tutorial.
