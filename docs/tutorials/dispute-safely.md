# Handle a dispute without losing the facts

Resolution · 4 min · External references checked 2026-09-12.

A dispute should freeze only the contested milestone while preserving the agreement, evidence and both parties’ responses.

## 1. Pause the right scope

Identify the exact milestone in question. Do not assume unrelated work is part of the dispute.

## 2. Build a timeline

Keep delivery references, messages, amendments and evidence in chronological order.

## 3. Invite a response

Give the other party a clear question and a reasonable response window.

## 4. Apply the agreement

Use the written acceptance test and the remedy both sides accepted.

## 5. Record the outcome

Explain whether the result is release, refund, revision or continued hold.

## Important limitation

The dispute screen is a demonstration. It does not replace a staffed support process, legal advice or a real buyer-protection service.
