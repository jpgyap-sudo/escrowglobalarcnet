# Buy USDC or USDT with a card or bank

Payments · 4 min · External references checked 2026-09-12.

An on-ramp buys crypto into your own wallet. It does not fund an Escrow Global escrow or make a delivery acceptable.

## 1. Select your own Solana wallet

Use a wallet you control. Check the receiving address carefully. Never paste a seed phrase or private key into Escrow Global or a provider form.

## 2. Choose a token and provider

The Buy screen offers a configured MoonPay checkout and links to independent alternatives. Always select USDC or USDT specifically on Solana—not Ethereum, Tron or another network.

## 3. Verify with the provider

The provider may request identity verification, residency information and payment-method checks. Complete these on the provider’s official domain. Escrow Global does not process your card details or identity documents.

## 4. Review the final quote

Card, Apple Pay, Google Pay and bank availability depends on country, device, asset and provider approval. Compare the fiat total, provider fees, network costs and exact tokens received before confirming.

## 5. Confirm the deposit separately

Wait for the tokens to reach your own wallet. Then a production escrow would need a separate verified funding transaction. This demo has no live custody contract; do not send money to its demo identifiers.

## Important limitation

MoonPay sandbox requires partner test keys. Live MoonPay requires approval, network-specific currency codes, signed URLs and IP matching; those requirements cannot be replaced with a demo payment callback.
