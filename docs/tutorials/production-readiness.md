# Before you start an escrow

Start here · 3 min · External references checked 2026-09-12.

Use this quick checklist to understand a deal before you begin.

## 1. Know the deal

Read the item or service, price, asset, deadline, acceptance checks and available remedies.

## 2. Know your role

The deal room shows whether it is your turn to accept, fund, deliver, inspect or respond.

## 3. Check the other party

Confirm who you are dealing with and keep important questions inside the agreement record.

## 4. Protect your wallet

Use a wallet you control. Review every payment request and never share recovery words or private keys.

## 5. Start safely

Practice with the sandbox first. Do not send real tokens or personal documents to this demo.

## Important limitation

This website is an interactive sandbox. Balances and identities are examples only; no real funds move here. It is not a live escrow service, buyer-protection guarantee or source of legal or financial advice.
