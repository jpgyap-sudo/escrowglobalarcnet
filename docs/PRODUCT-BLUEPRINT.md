# Escrow Global Settlement product blueprint

**Positioning:** turn a discovered listing, an existing relationship or a private agreement into clear payment milestones. A services marketplace is one entrance; product procurement and bring-your-own-client contracts use the same payment logic.

These categories are the proposed supported scope, not a claim that all are legally permitted in every country. The delivered product checkout specializes in goods; some other categories use a structured generic milestone agreement rather than domain-specific asset transfer automation.

## Eight reusable workflows

Physical goods; development projects; marketing deliverables; professional services; private contracts; procurement; warranty/holdbacks; digital handovers. Each template prompts for missing terms. The custom-agreement UI creates two explicitly specified stages; the underlying model supports up to eight.

The contract builder is deterministic structured input, **not an AI model that decides completion**. Acceptance is an original party action, a permitted simulation timeout, mutual settlement or dispute decision. No AI or courier can silently set a funded agreement to “green.”

## A launch wedge worth testing

Start with a tightly supported combination of software/creative services and specification-heavy product orders. The bring-your-own-client path makes the product useful before its public discovery marketplace has meaningful demand. A later sourcing/inspection workflow could suit furniture and supplier procurement, but it needs trained evidence review and actual logistics integrations rather than broad promises of buyer protection.

Useful differentiation already modeled: milestone-isolated disagreements; free normal revisions/returns before formal escalation; two-party scope resets; fee reserve refunded on unused principal; original wallet destinations; explicit warranty protection remaining. Features not built but worth testing later: saved acceptance-checklist libraries, verified independent inspectors, reusable supplier contracts, auditable completion receipts and B2B API integrations.

## Marketing-specific caution

Escrow can protect payment for agreed work. It cannot make promised sales, impressions or conversion rates true. Distinguish an output milestone from an outcome guarantee. Define the authorized analytics source, baseline, attribution period, organic/paid mix and exclusions before any outcome-based agreement. Do not admit fake engagement, deceptive promotion, hidden endorsements or market-manipulation services.

## Trust and exclusions

Do not admit illegal goods/services, regulated weapons, stolen/counterfeit assets, prohibited financial schemes, laundering or fabricated identities. High-risk categories such as titled real estate, regulated professional client funds, investments and money transmission require separate analysis and are not operational categories of this demo. An application can escrow payment without possessing or legally transferring the underlying asset.

Reports and blocking are available, but production requires identity/account integrity, moderation staffing, sanctions/AML decisions where applicable, ownership verification, complaint handling, privacy protection and a published response/appeal process. No reputation NFT, platform token or yield strategy is necessary for the initial product.
