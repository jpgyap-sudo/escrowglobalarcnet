READ BEFORE CONNECTING OR SIGNING

Blockchain gives you control. It also gives you responsibility.

This notice explains the risks of smart contracts, wallets and public blockchains. Escrow Global cannot promise that code, networks, providers, websites or counterparties will be safe, available or recoverable.

If Escrow Global is under attack or something looks wrong: stop.

Do not connect a wallet, sign a message, approve a token, follow a support link or send funds. Close the page and use only an independently verified official domain or a known support channel. A warning on this page cannot make an unsafe transaction safe.

01

Smart-contract and code risk

Programs can contain bugs, exploitable logic, unsafe upgrades, administrator controls or dependencies such as oracles and third-party services. An audit, published source code or a familiar brand is not a guarantee. A program may behave differently from what you expect, and a loss may be permanent.

02

Network and transaction risk

Transactions can fail, be delayed, be reordered or become expensive during congestion. Confirmed blockchain activity is generally difficult or impossible to reverse. Check the exact network, token mint, destination, amount, fees and finality before signing. The same ticker on another network is not the same asset.

03

Wallet and phishing risk

A malicious website, browser extension, QR code or support impersonator can request a dangerous approval or signature. Never share a seed phrase, private key, recovery code or password. A wallet connection is not proof that a website is legitimate, and a signature request is not “just a login” unless you have verified what it does.

04

Platform, provider and counterparty risk

Escrow Global may be unavailable, compromised, impersonated or unable to recover a loss. External providers, arbiters, RPCs, bridges, exchanges and counterparties can fail or act incorrectly. Escrow Global does not guarantee custody, solvency, delivery, dispute outcomes, security or uninterrupted access.

YOUR WALLET SAFETY PLAN

Use a separate wallet for Escrow Global

Create a dedicated wallet used only for Escrow Global activity. Keep only the amount you intend to use there. Do not connect your main savings wallet, treasury wallet or a wallet containing assets you cannot afford to lose.

  • Verify the domain. Use a bookmark you created yourself and check the spelling and HTTPS origin before connecting.
  • Review every request. Match the program, network, mint, amount, recipient and permissions in your wallet before signing.
  • Keep recovery secrets offline. Store seed phrases and private keys securely. Escrow Global support will never ask for them.
  • Limit approvals and balances. Revoke unused approvals through a trusted tool and keep only minimal funds in the dedicated wallet.
  • Pause on pressure. Urgent messages, guaranteed returns, “recovery” offers and last-minute address changes are warning signs.
